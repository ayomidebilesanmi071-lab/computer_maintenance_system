Guidance

Choosing a priority

  • Critical — the equipment cannot be used and it blocks work (for example a server or the main switch).
  • High — a user cannot do their job, and there is no workaround.
  • Medium — work continues but is slowed or impaired.
  • Low — cosmetic or minor inconvenience.

What happens next

The fault is recorded with the status Reported. An ICT officer acknowledges it, assigns it if needed, records the repair in the maintenance history and then resolves and closes it. Every status change is stored together with who made it and when.
