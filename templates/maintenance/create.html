How outcomes affect the asset

  • Completed - Equipment Working → the asset returns to In Use.
  • Completed - Equipment Not Fully Working → the asset is marked Faulty.
  • Unsuccessful - Fault Persists → the asset stays Faulty.
  • Deferred - Awaiting Parts → the asset is marked Under Maintenance.

Retired equipment is never moved back into service automatically. Every maintenance entry, condition change and status change is written to the audit trail in the same database transaction.
